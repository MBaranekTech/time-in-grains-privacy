Time in Grains
Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Overview

Time in Grains is an Android home-screen widget app. Widgets, personal goals, calendar import and background-image processing work on your device. The app has no app account, developer-operated server or advertising integration.

The Google Play edition includes optional, one-time support purchases through Google Play Billing. Core widget and goal features work without purchasing anything and remain available offline. A separate offline edition does not include Google Play Billing or third-party runtime SDKs and does not declare the Android INTERNET permission.

Widget settings and personal goals

The app uses your phone's clock, time zone, locale and clock-format preference to calculate calendar progress. It saves widget appearance, selected periods, colors and layout choices in private on-device storage. No birth date is requested.

Personal goals store the name you enter, start time, deadline and appearance settings locally. Goal names and dates are visible on your home screen when you add a goal widget. Goal percentages measure elapsed time rather than completed work. Our app code does not send goal names, dates or widget settings to the developer or Google Play Billing.

Optional calendar access

Manual goals require no calendar permission. Calendar import asks for the optional Android READ_CALENDAR permission only after you choose the calendar-access action. With permission, the picker reads event titles, occurrence start and end times, all-day flags, calendar IDs and calendar display names from the phone's local calendar provider for the range you choose. This can include events previously synced by your calendar app.

The app uses this information locally to browse, filter and review events. It copies only the selected title and chosen progress dates into a goal, with a flag that the goal was imported. Temporary screen state may retain the selected event's title, dates, calendar label and filter choices to restore an interrupted review. Event identifiers, descriptions, attendees and locations are not saved; descriptions, attendees and locations are not read.

The app does not write calendar events, request calendar sync or connect to calendar services. Later changes in your calendar are not automatically copied to an imported goal. Revoke calendar permission in Android app settings at any time. Saved imported goals keep their copied information until you edit or delete them.

Optional background images

When you choose a background, the Android image picker grants access to the selected local image without photo-library or storage permission. The app resizes it to at most 768 pixels on its longest edge and saves a re-encoded JPEG copy in private app storage. Source image metadata is not copied. Blur is calculated on your device.

Our app code does not upload images, scan your photo library or read the system wallpaper. Unsaved image drafts use temporary cache files. Removing a background and saving deletes that widget's saved copy; other widgets may keep independent copies.

Optional Google Play support purchases

In the Google Play edition, opening Support connects to Google Play to request available products, localized prices and outstanding purchases. If an unfinished purchase has been recorded, reopening the app also checks it with Google Play. Google handles checkout, payment details and the associated Google account and transaction information under Google's terms and privacy policy.

The app receives product IDs, purchase status, package name, purchase tokens and related purchase fields in memory to confirm and consume support purchases. It does not save card details, donor profiles, order history or lifetime totals. A private on-device flag records whether an unfinished purchase needs another check. Pending purchases are not treated as completed support.

Google's Billing SDK and its Google dependencies may communicate technical app, device, service and diagnostic information to Google. The offline edition's no-network description does not apply to the Google Play edition. No goal titles, calendar events or background images are passed by our app code to Billing. Any transaction or payout records Google makes available to the developer are used to manage purchases, refunds, support and applicable accounting obligations. Google controls payment-record retention under its policies.

Storage, retention and deletion

Goals, widget preferences and selected image copies are stored in the app's private Android storage. Android backup is disabled, and data extraction rules exclude private preferences and image files from cloud backup and device transfer. The app does not operate a developer cloud copy of these items.

Delete a goal in the app to remove its saved name, dates and appearance. Existing goal widgets then ask you to choose another goal. Removing a goal widget keeps the saved goal in your library. Removing a widget deletes its own style preferences and image copy. Independent default settings and default images remain until changed or removed, app storage is cleared, or the app is uninstalled.

Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the app's locally stored goals, settings and images. Android may clear temporary cache files separately. There is no app account to delete. Clearing app storage does not remove Google's payment records or request a purchase refund.

Widget placement requests and receipts are stored locally and expire when a later request cleans up entries older than one day. Diagnostic device logs may contain placement status and widget IDs, but not goal names or goal dates.

Permissions and your choices

READ_CALENDAR is optional and read-only. RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED restores scheduled widget updates after a phone restart. The Google Play edition also includes permissions supplied by Google Play Billing and its dependencies. The app does not request calendar-write, contacts, microphone, camera or device-location permissions to provide widgets.

You can use manual goals without calendar access, remove a chosen image, delete saved goals or clear app storage. You can decline support purchases without losing widget features. Android and your launcher control home-screen placement and may delay background refreshes.
